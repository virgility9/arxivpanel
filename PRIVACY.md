# Privacy Policy — Arxiv

Arxiv is a student paper-library app. This policy explains what the app collects, where it is stored, and what you can do about it.

What we collect
• Your account: the email address, username, and avatar you register with. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication — the app never sees or stores it.
• Your papers: the title, abstract, field, and PDF you submit, plus the cover image made from the PDF's first page.
• Your activity: comments you post, papers you like or save, your reading history, and your theme choice. Admins can see announcements you have read, so the Contest tab can clear its notification badge.

Where it is stored
• Account, papers, comments, likes, and settings live in Firebase (Google) — Authentication and Firestore.
• Paper PDFs live on Cloudinary, on its free tier. A PDF you submit is uploaded there so other users can read it.
• Preset avatars come from DiceBear. Your own photo, if you pick one, is stored in your profile as an image, not sent anywhere else.

What we do not do
• No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no selling or sharing your data with anyone else.
• Papers you submit are reviewed by an admin before they appear in the Local Library. Rejected papers are not published.

Your choices
• You can edit your profile, delete your own papers, and sign out at any time.
• If you want your account deleted, ask an admin. Deleting the profile stops the account signing back in; the sign-in record itself is removed by the developer in the Firebase console.
• If you use the app while signed out, you can browse public papers, but liking, saving, commenting, and submitting need an account.

Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will appear here in the app. Questions about your data can be raised through the project page: github.com/virgility9/arxivpanel
